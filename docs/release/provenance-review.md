# Source and artwork scope

Original source, rack definitions and artwork are maintained as project material under AGPL-3.0-only, with separate dependency/font terms. Preserve all genuine contributor and third-party notices. Source/hash identity is not a universal ownership or privacy guarantee.

The native build embeds the editable PNG/SVG, control/layout JSON and fonts supplied under resources. [Editing notes](../design/README.md) explain those inputs. Private design comparisons, research and approval transcripts are not required by the product build and are not public payloads. No commercial reference-plugin binary, decompiled source, model weight or external sample pack is supplied.

Earlier bounded reviews inspected original source/artwork provenance and metadata; they did not claim independent clean-room derivation or full-size human review of every pixel. Changed material requires a fresh bounded review. The twelve starter sounds use only ordinary controls of the existing built-in racks and contain no samples.

The existing ShortRoom response was explicitly cleared by the maintainer while its original generation/import record remained unrecovered. Its bytes and saved-project compatibility were retained. This is a disclosed provenance limit, not a new recovered origin claim.

Final source-tree, notice/privacy scans, reproducible build, install, host and listening results must bind to the actual release. This document does not itself approve publication.
