# Community conduct

air welcomes contributors and testers of different backgrounds, abilities and levels of experience. Be respectful, give specific constructive feedback, credit other people's work and respect a person's decision not to share private information or recordings.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual attention, publishing personal information without permission and repeated disruption are not acceptable. Disagree with a proposal without attacking its author. These expectations apply in project issues, pull requests and other spaces maintained for the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to [support@planetary.music](mailto:support@planetary.music), with the subject **air conduct report**. Include relevant links and only the personal information needed to explain the concern. This uses the existing support address; delivery and response times are not guaranteed during alpha preparation.

The maintainer reviews concerns and may clarify expectations, remove content, restrict participation or ban an account according to severity and repeated behavior. They will limit disclosure of a report to what is needed to address it, while making no promise of absolute confidentiality. Anyone involved in a concern should disclose that conflict and avoid deciding their own case where another maintainer can review it.

This is the project's own conduct policy. It does not create a legal service agreement or an obligation to accept a contribution.
